MAIN FEATURES (where to find them)
- Chat: "Chat" tab. 1:1 and group messages, images. Friends are added with a yuID (My Page -> "Your yuID"; the other person must approve the request). Strangers cannot message you.
- YuSnap: "YuSnap" tab. One front-camera photo per day, visible to friends only. A push notification arrives once a day at a random time; to try it, tap the camera on the YuSnap tab when it is available.
- Games: "Play" tab. Board and card games (Othello, Shogi, Daifugo, Yacht, etc.). Rooms are joined with a 5-digit room number.
- Notifications: My Page -> "Turn on notifications".

ABOUT THE "COINS"
- "yuCoin" is a virtual in-app point only. It cannot be bought with money, cannot be cashed out, and cannot be exchanged for prizes. The app has no in-app purchases and no real-money gambling.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT SAFEGUARDS (Guideline 1.2)
- Terms of use agreement is required on first launch.
- Objectionable-word filter on messages, group names, display names and YuSnap captions.
- Report button on messages, YuSnap posts and users; block button (My Page -> blocked list). Reports are reviewed by the operator, who removes content and users when needed.
- Account deletion: My Page -> scroll to the bottom -> "Delete account" (type your name to confirm).

- Terms: https://yuchin0809.github.io/yuchat/terms.html / Privacy: https://yuchin0809.github.io/yuchat/privacy.html

TECHNICAL NOTE
- This is a hybrid app: the interface is served from https://yuchin0809.github.io/yuchat/ and uses Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging). Native features used: push notifications and the camera.
